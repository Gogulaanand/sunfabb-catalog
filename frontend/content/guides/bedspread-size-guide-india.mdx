Most bedspreads that arrive too small were bought against US or UK sizing. **Indian bed sizes are not the same as American or British ones** - an Indian queen is narrower and shorter than an American queen, and an Indian king is a different shape again.

This guide gives the Indian standards, then the arithmetic for turning a mattress measurement into the bedspread size you actually need.

## Indian mattress standards

| Bed | Inches | Centimetres | Feet |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single | 36 x 72 | 91 x 183 | 3 x 6 |
| Single (long) | 36 x 75 | 91 x 190 | 3 x 6.25 |
| Double / Diwan | 48 x 72 | 122 x 183 | 4 x 6 |
| Queen | 60 x 78 | 152 x 198 | 5 x 6.5 |
| King | 72 x 78 | 183 x 198 | 6 x 6.5 |
| King (extra long) | 72 x 84 | 183 x 213 | 6 x 7 |

Two things to note. Indian beds are commonly quoted in feet, and the second number is usually 6 ft or 6.5 ft rather than the 6.67 ft (80 in) standard in the US. Custom-made beds from a local carpenter frequently sit a few inches off any of these - always measure rather than assume.

## How Indian sizes compare internationally

| Size name | India | US | UK |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single | 36 x 72 | 39 x 75 | 36 x 75 |
| Double | 48 x 72 | 54 x 75 | 54 x 75 |
| Queen | 60 x 78 | 60 x 80 | 60 x 78 |
| King | 72 x 78 | 76 x 80 | 72 x 78 |

An imported "queen" bedspread sized for a US bed will be about 2 inches longer than an Indian queen needs - harmless. An imported US "double" is 6 inches wider than an Indian double - which shows, because the drop becomes uneven.

## Calculating the size you need

A bedspread must cover the mattress **and** hang down each side. That hang is called the **drop**.

**Bedspread width = mattress width + (2 x drop)**

**Bedspread length = mattress length + drop + tuck allowance**

Only one drop is added to the length, because the head of the bed sits against a headboard. Add a **tuck allowance of 10-12 inches** at the foot if you want to tuck the spread under the mattress or fold it over pillows.

### Choosing a drop

Measure your mattress height first - Indian mattresses run 5-10 inches, and a bed with a mattress topper can reach 12.

| Look | Drop | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coverlet | 6-8 in | Sits above the bed frame, shows the base |
| Standard | 10-14 in | Covers the mattress and a little of the frame |
| Full | 15-18 in | Covers mattress and frame, stops above the floor |
| Floor-length | Mattress height + frame height | Skirts to the floor, hides storage drawers |

For a **divan or storage bed**, a floor-length drop is usually the point - it hides the box. For a bed with decorative wooden legs, a standard drop looks intentional and a floor-length one buries the feature you paid for.

## Worked examples

**Indian queen, 8 in mattress, standard drop of 12 in, no tuck**

- Width: 60 + (2 x 12) = **84 in**
- Length: 78 + 12 = **90 in**
- Buy: 90 x 90 in, or the nearest size up

**Indian king, 10 in mattress, full drop of 16 in, with a 10 in pillow tuck**

- Width: 72 + (2 x 16) = **104 in**
- Length: 78 + 16 + 10 = **104 in**
- Buy: 108 x 108 in

**Indian single, 6 in mattress, coverlet drop of 8 in**

- Width: 36 + (2 x 8) = **52 in**
- Length: 72 + 8 = **80 in**
- Buy: 60 x 90 in, the common single bedspread size

## Practical rules

- **Measure the mattress, not the bed frame.** Frames vary far more than mattresses.
- **Measure with the bedding you actually use.** A mattress protector plus a topper can add 3 inches of height, which comes straight off your drop.
- **When between sizes, size up.** Excess fabric drapes; a short bedspread never looks anything but short.
- **Allow for shrinkage.** Cotton loses 2-3% on first wash. On a 108 in bedspread that is up to 3 inches - real enough to matter if you sized exactly.
- **Check both numbers.** Many listings quote only one dimension. A "90 inch bedspread" could be 90 x 90 or 90 x 108, and those fit very different beds.
- **For twin beds pushed together**, treat them as one bed of combined width; two single spreads leave a visible gap down the middle.

## Quick answers

- **What size bedspread for an Indian queen bed?** 90 x 108 inches covers a 60 x 78 in queen with a standard drop.
- **Is an Indian king the same as a US king?** No. Indian king is 72 x 78 in; US king is 76 x 80 in.
- **How much drop should a bedspread have?** 10-14 inches for a standard look; more if you want to hide a storage base.
- **Can I use a double bedspread on a queen bed?** Not well - a queen is 12 inches wider, which removes almost all the drop on both sides.
